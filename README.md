# SIERRA Dataset: LLM-Generated Environmental Sustainability Indicators for Tourism POIs

This repository contains the dataset, prompt template and output schema used in the paper:

> **SIERRA: A Framework for Evaluating the Reliability of LLM-Generated Sustainability Data for Tourism Points of Interest**
> MY. Salmony and A. Moreno. *[Journal name, year — to be completed after publication]*

> **Note:** The dataset will also be available soon in the **URV institutional repository**. The link will be added here once available.

## About the paper

Reliable sustainability data for tourism Points of Interest (POIs) are essential for sustainability-aware recommender systems, but such data are rarely available at the level of individual POIs. Large Language Models (LLMs) can generate these data, yet their reliability is difficult to assess without ground truth.

The paper introduces **SIERRA** (*Sustainability Indicator Evaluation via Reliability and Robustness Analysis*), a framework for evaluating the reliability of LLM-generated sustainability indicators in the absence of ground truth. SIERRA combines five reliability dimensions:

1. Internal coherence
2. Cross-model consistency
3. Distributional robustness
4. Variability and disagreement analysis
5. Reliability-based model selection

It also includes a downstream robustness analysis based on TOPSIS rankings.

SIERRA is applied to **seven environmental sustainability indicators** generated by **nine LLMs** for **240 tourism POIs** in Barcelona across six categories. The results show that the generated data are structurally coherent, that resource-consumption indicators are more reliable than the more subjective ones, and that reliability-aware model selection and ensemble aggregation produce robust sustainability rankings.

## Indicators

| Indicator | Format |
|---|---|
| Water Consumption (Score) | Score in [0, 1] |
| Water Consumption (Actual) | Estimated litres per day |
| CO₂ Emissions (Score) | Score in [0, 1] |
| CO₂ Emissions (Actual) | Estimated kg per day |
| Noise Level | Ordinal, 1 (very low) to 5 (very high) |
| Pollution Control | Score in [0, 1] |
| Biodiversity Protection | Score in [0, 1] |

## Evaluated LLMs

Claude-Haiku-4.5, GPT-4.1-mini, GPT-5.4-mini, GPT-5.4-nano, Ministral-3 14B, GPT-OSS 20B, DeepSeek-R1 14B, Phi-4 14B and Qwen3 14B.
